Cards with game placeholders: before and after the fix

Real data and the game's colours and icons (cut from its icon sheets). Before: the game builds a description in pieces around each placeholder, and the mod pasted the whole card into every piece, so the card appeared twice around the key icon and the placeholder stayed raw. After: the card is built once, the game draws the key icon, and the key part sits on its own line under its effect. Key caps are stand-ins: B is the default for Glacial Domain/Laser (from your screenshot); other keys show the action name, the game shows your own binding.
